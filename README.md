# EDS223-HW1

This repository uses tmap to create a map of Air Toxin Cancer Risk in New Orleans and another map of Diesel Particulate Matter Risk in New Orleans.

"The_Code.qmd" has all the code chunks to create said maps.

# Data

Data comes from https://pedp-ejscreen.azurewebsites.net/, an unofficial copy of EJScreen, which is a tool created by the United States Environmental Protection Agency to map out Environmental Injustices across the United States. 

Authors: Liam Sarmiento

# Map explanation

The first map shows diesel particulate matter exposure risk in New Orleans, where the brighter yellow areas show higher exposure, while the second map shows cancer risk from air toxins in New Orleans, where darker maroon shows higher percentile of cancer risk. Comparing the two maps, there is strong correlation of diesel particulate matter exposure to increased risk of cancer. These maps demonstrate just one of many environmental injustice problems where populations who are exposed to more pollution are at higher risk of developing health problems.
